## Key Features & Visualizations
* **Geographic Map (Covid Testing and Outcomes):** State-level breakdown tracking regional hospitalization and death percentages across the United States.
* **Time-Series Trends (Hospitalizations and Deaths over time):** Longitudinal tracking of cumulative hospitalizations and deaths from early 2020 through early 2021.
* **Metric Breakdown (Hospitalizations and Deaths by Location):** Comparative bar charts highlighting volume distribution by state.
* **Test Results (Positive vs Negative Tests):** Proportional distribution showing the scale of positive, negative, and total test results nationwide.
